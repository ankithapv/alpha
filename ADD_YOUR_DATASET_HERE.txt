DATASET UPDATE GUIDE

1. Your real, verified records should be entered into the Google Sheet tabs.
2. Keep the sheet names and row-1 headers exactly as documented in README.md.
3. The Excel template supplied by your instructor is copied into this folder as a reference.
4. Do not paste private student information into GitHub. The .gitignore excludes credentials and .env.
5. The backend reads your live Google Sheet when DEMO_MODE=false.

The mapping is in app/sheets.py -> SHEET_HEADERS.
